# Campaign TOW

Campaign TOW is a web app built to follow a **Warhammer: The Old World** campaign played at the Launa'Gamers club in Launaguet, France.

Live instance: **https://old-world-campaign.ben-lds.com**

The project turns battle results, experience gains, permanent injuries, territories, and unit progression into a shared campaign history.

---

## Why It Exists

A narrative campaign quickly creates a lot of shared state: evolving armies, experienced units, injured characters, contested territories, and power gaps between players.

Campaign TOW keeps that memory in one place without replacing the end-of-game table ritual. Players still discuss, calculate, and agree on outcomes together. The app then becomes the shared campaign log that everyone can consult later.

---

## What It Does

- Tracks each army's evolution across the campaign.
- Shows units, experience gains, tier progression, and lasting injuries.
- Records post-battle consequences after a game.
- Keeps a readable timeline of matches and important changes.
- Lets players browse opposing armies before future games.
- Centralizes useful campaign rules for the group.

The campaign rules are still evolving and will be shared publicly once stabilized.

---

## Design Principles

- **Memory, not referee.** The app records player decisions; it does not replace agreement around the table.
- **Trust between participants.** The project is designed for a closed group of players who know each other.
- **Narrative chronicle.** An army's evolution should read like a story, not just a spreadsheet.
- **Fast reference.** During a game, important information should stay easy to reach.

---

## Who It Is For

Campaign TOW is primarily for the players of the Launa'Gamers campaign.

This repository may also be useful to people interested in how a narrative wargame campaign can be supported by a dedicated web app.
